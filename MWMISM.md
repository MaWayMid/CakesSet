# A Speech On The Meaning of Life

### Intro
The purpose of life biologically is to first make sure you are alive then deliver your genetic material. Biologically, the key of life purpose is to deliver your genetic material to the next generation. But for people nowadays, maybe the purpose of life is more focusing on the living a life itself.  
### Masterpiece of Nature, Consciousness
The main reason why humans are different from other animals is that humans can think, can talk, we have consciousness.  
In 2025, so far, we have little knowledge about how it work, how it form, how it disappear and many questions related to it.  
So what do we know so far? Well, let's gather some basic information.  
First, we all born with consciousness that's unique. And we are who we are, we cannot change into somebody else.  
Second, death is the end of the status of having consciousness, so far there hasn't got any evidence that there exists a afterlife world, or you will turn into someone else after death.
