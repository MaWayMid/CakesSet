# The Last Gernelts
Good evening, dear citizens and friends,  
晚上好，市民和公民朋友们：  
As the prisident of Gernelts Republic, the latest development of the whole situation is way more out of our expectation. I declare to resign my position as the prisident as Gernelts Republic. Though, I insist in discharging my duty as prisident.
